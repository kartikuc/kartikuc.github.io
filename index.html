Kartik Chikkanagoudar

About

I'm a final-year Dual Degree student in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, and I've spent most of that time falling further down the rabbit hole of solid state devices — the stuff of nanoelectronics, semiconductor physics, and device technology, chased through both simulation and the messier reality of the lab bench. I like problems that make you sit with a material's physics until it finally clicks: how vacancies move through an oxide, why a transistor's threshold drifts, what a 2D crystal actually does under bias.

That curiosity spills into a few adjacent corners too — III-nitride devices, 2D materials, photonics and neuromorphic computing, TCAD and compact modeling — less a checklist and more the set of things I keep getting pulled back to.

A lot of my energy these days goes into HackerFab IIT Bombay, which I co-founded to make hands-on chip fabrication something students here can actually get their hands dirty with, not just read about.

Outside the lab, I enjoy photography — a few shots are up on my photobook.
