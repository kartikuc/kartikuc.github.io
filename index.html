About

Final-year Dual Degree student in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, specializing in Solid State Devices with a minor in Computer Science. Research spans nanoelectronics, semiconductor physics, and device technology — both theoretical and experimental.

Co-founder of HackerFab IIT Bombay, working to make hands-on chip fabrication accessible across India.

Outside the lab, I enjoy photography — a few shots are up on my photobook.

I also tinker with small side-projects for fun: a Game of Life sim, a Game BYOB build, and a few other odds and ends — all collected under /FunStuff.
